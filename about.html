About Me

A little about the collector behind Stewy’s Cards.

My Story

I’m Brandon, and at heart, I’m a collector.

I’m a proud father of three, and one of my favorite things is collecting Pokémon with my kids. It’s something we can enjoy together, share memories through, and continue to discover along the way. What started as a passion for collecting has grown into something much bigger — a way to meet people, share memories, and be part of an amazing community.

For me, Pokémon is about more than the cards. It’s about the friendships, conversations, trades, events, and connections made along the way. Every collection has a story, and I love being part of the stories that bring collectors together.

Stewy’s Cards is built around that passion — collecting, connecting, and enjoying the hobby together.
